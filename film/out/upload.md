## Title
DISCIPLINE: Why You Keep Starting Over (Animated Short Film)

## Alternate title
He Started "Day 1" 47 Times. Here's What Was Stopping Him.

## Description
Leo has restarted Day 1 forty-seven times.

Not because he's lazy. Every time, a voice in his head talked him out of it. It never said "never." It said "five more minutes," "watch one more video first" and "the week's ruined, start Monday."

Then, at 6 AM one morning, he found out something nobody tells you about the people who actually show up.

This short film is for anyone who has bought the new planner, watched the 5 AM routine video, and then missed the gym on Tuesday.

Three rules from the film:
1. Don't ask. Count 5 to 1.
2. Make it stupid small. One push-up. One page.
3. Never miss twice.
