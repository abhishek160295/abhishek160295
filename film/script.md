# DISCIPLINE — an animated short

**Runtime:** 3:24 · 1920×1080 · 24 fps

## Premise

**Core truth.** People think disciplined people *feel* like doing it. They don't. Nobody feels like it; the disciplined ones just stopped asking the feeling for permission.

**The villain: "Monday."** It's the voice that negotiates. It never says *never*. It says *later*, and that's how it wins. Its lines: *Five more minutes. Watch one more first. Start Monday. You earned this. It's not good enough. Plan it perfectly first.*

**The counter:** `TIMES HE STARTED OVER: 47`
- It climbs 0 → 47 while the villain wins.
- At the twist it turns into `DAYS MAYA FELT LIKE RUNNING: 9 / 212`. Leo learns the person he thought was "naturally disciplined" almost never feels like it either.
- In the montage it freezes at 47 and turns white. On day 10 it tries to tick to 48 and fails.
- The film ends on it alone on black, still 47.

**Cast**
- **Leo:** cream bean, the hero.
- **Monday:** Leo's shadow. Black, red halo, red slit eyes. Its size equals its power.
- **Maya:** mint beanie. Her lines are mint. She has her own small shadow.
- **Sam:** orange beanie. A new kid at the bar, with a tiny shadow.

## Script

### COLD OPEN (0:00–0:10)

**01 · THE WALL (0:00–6.5).** Night, Leo's room. The red clock flips 23:59 → 00:00 and reads MON. A wall is covered in yellow sticky notes, every one saying DAY 1. Slow push-in. Leo writes one more note and slaps it on the wall. The HUD ticks `46 → 47`, in red.
> CAPTION: *Day 1. Again.*

**02 · REVEAL (6.5–10).** Black. A low tritone drone. Two red slits open in the dark.
> CARD: *Here's what stopped him.*

### THE DAY (0:10–1:05). One year earlier. Six restarts, compressed.

**03 · SNOOZE (10–19).** 05:30, gym bag packed by the door. The villain sits on the edge of the bed and presses snooze for him. The clock skips to 07:48 and Leo jolts up. The counter goes 0 → 1.
> CAPTION: *One year earlier.*
> VILLAIN: *Five more minutes.*

**04 · RESEARCH (19–28).** At the desk. The textbook stays shut while the laptop plays "HOW TO BE DISCIPLINED (10 TIPS)", then "Up next: 5AM ROUTINE OF A CEO". The clock goes 19:00 → 23:40. 1 → 6.
> VILLAIN: *Watch one more first.*

**05 · TUESDAY (28–37).** Whip pan to a rainy street with a lit GYM sign. Leo's phone reads TUESDAY. He stops, then turns around. 6 → 14.
> VILLAIN: *Week's ruined. Start Monday.*

**06 · EARNED (37–46).** Kitchen at 23:10. Prepped meals labelled MON TUE WED in the fridge. The doorbell rings and a pizza box arrives. 14 → 23.
> VILLAIN: *You earned this.*

**07 · DRAFT (46–55).** His first guitar cover, ready to upload. The cursor hovers over POST, then: *Delete draft? → Yes.* 23 → 35.
> VILLAIN: *It's not good enough.*

**08 · THE PLAN (55–65).** A perfect colour-coded schedule goes up on the wall and the villain nods along. A hard cut to Monday morning: the plan is untouched and Leo is on the couch. 35 → 47.
> VILLAIN: *Plan it perfectly first.*

### THE LOW POINT (1:05–1:18)

**09 · 02:47 (65–78).** Rain on the window. Leo sits on the edge of his bed. The villain is at full size and doesn't fit in the frame. One second of silence.
> **VILLAIN (SPOKEN, distorted):** *"You're just not built for this."*

One second of silence.
> CAPTION: *He'd never talk to a friend like this.* (silence) *Only to himself.*

### THE TWIST (1:18–1:48)

**10 · 6:02 AM (78–86).** Cold blue dawn. Leo couldn't sleep and walks with his villain looming behind him. Across the street, Maya stands at her door in running shoes, looking wrecked.
> CAPTION: *6:02 AM. He couldn't sleep.*

**11 · HER SHADOW (86–96).** The music cuts to silence. Behind Maya, two small red eyes open: she has one too. Her shadow says *"Stay in bed."* Maya sighs, ties her shoe and runs anyway. Her shadow shrinks. Leo's villain freezes and glitches.

**12 · THE QUESTION (96–108).**
> LEO: *You hear it too?*
> MAYA (mint): *Every morning.*
> LEO: *Then how do you still go?*
> MAYA: *I stopped asking it.*

The HUD glitches and rewrites itself as `DAYS MAYA FELT LIKE RUNNING: 9 / 212`, in mint.

### BLACK CARDS (1:48–2:00)

**13.**
> *Disciplined people don't feel like it either.*
> **(red)** *They just stopped asking.*

### THE TURN + MONTAGE (2:00–2:40)

**14 · 05:30 AGAIN (120–130).** Same framing as scene 03. The alarm goes off and the villain is big again. One second of silence.
> **VILLAIN (SPOKEN):** *"Just start Monday."*

One second of silence. Leo's eyes open.
> CAPTION: *5… 4… 3… 2… 1.*

He sits up and the villain shrinks.
> LABEL: **1. DON'T ASK. COUNT 5 TO 1.**

**15 · RULES (130–137).**
> LABEL: **2. MAKE IT STUPID SMALL.** One push-up. One page.
> LABEL: **3. NEVER MISS TWICE.** A calendar with one red X, followed by an unbroken line of ticks.

**16 · MONTAGE (137–160).** Cut on the beat. Red sweatband on. `DAY 1 → DAY 180`. The villain shrinks every cut, and every red line it throws shatters into sparks against Leo.
- DAY 1: one push-up. *Pointless.*
- DAY 2: one page. *You're tired.*
- DAY 3: running in the rain. *It's raining.*
- DAY 5: phone in the drawer. *Check your phone.*
- DAY 7: squats. *Everyone's staring.*
- DAY 9: sick in bed, a red X.
- DAY 10: one push-up anyway. *Missed one. Quit.* The counter flickers to 48 in red and snaps back to 47.
- DAY 14: stairs. *Too hard.*
- DAY 21: cooking. *Order pizza.*
- DAY 30: the cover is POSTED. *Not good enough.*
- DAYS 45, 60, 75 (running with Maya), 90, 100, 120, 150.
- DAY 180: the top of the stairs at dawn.

### THE PAYOFF (2:40–3:05)

**17 · THE WALL, AGAIN (160–171).** A match cut to the cold open: same room, same framing. Leo sweeps the 47 DAY 1 notes off the wall, writes one new note, **DAY 181**, and slaps it up with the same gesture as before.
> CAPTION: *He never needed another Day 1.*

**18 · PASS IT ON (171–185).** The park bars at dusk. Sam freezes under the pull-up bar while a tiny shadow whispers *"Everyone's watching."* Leo walks over.
> LEO: *Just do one. That's it.*

Sam does one, and his shadow breaks into red dust.
> CAPTION: *Now it was his turn to say it.*

### ENDING (3:05–3:24)

**19 · SUNRISE HILL (185–193).** Leo and Maya sit on top of the hill. Far behind and below them, the villain is a tiny dot with two red pinpricks.
> CAPTION: *The voice never fully goes away.* / *He just stopped asking it for permission.*

**20 · TITLE (193–200).** **DISCIPLINE**, with a red brush underline swoosh.
> *Motivation asks how you feel.*
> **(red)** *Discipline doesn't ask.*
> (grey) *What's the line your villain always says? Comment it.*

**21 · LOOP (200–204).** `TIMES HE STARTED OVER: 47` alone on black. It flickers red toward 48, then settles back to white 47. Cut to black.

## Sound

- **Theme:** a Karplus-Strong guitar in A minor. It starts as single thin plucks, adds bass notes and piano by the twist, and ends as full strummed chords with pads and piano for the payoff and hill.
- **Villain:** a dissonant tritone drone (A + D#) under every appearance. Its gain follows the villain's size.
- **Silence:** real silence before the twist (scene 11) and for one second on each side of the two spoken lines.
- **Foley:** alarm beeps, heartbeat at the low point, rain, crowd murmur at the gym, footsteps, thuds and risers into the cards, sparks on every shatter, and a doorbell.

## v2 revisions

- **The villain appears only when it's causing the problem.** It rises out of Leo's own shadow at the moment of temptation and sinks back into him afterwards. A red glow at his feet warns that it's coming. On the dawn walk it's hidden, showing only as two red pinpricks in his shadow. It dissolves into embers when Maya says *"I stopped asking it."*
- **Transitions:** a VHS rewind into "one year earlier", whip pans, a zoom-through into the laptop and into Maya, dips to black for the passing of time, a glitch into the truth cards, a flash cut into the montage, and a dissolve for the match cut. Montage cuts get a punch-in and a white flicker on each beat.
- **Labels:** every failure is tagged `RESTART #n · NAME` (THE ALARM, THE RESEARCH, THE MISSED MONDAY, THE REWARD, THE DRAFT, THE PERFECT PLAN). The rules are plates with a big red numeral and a one-line reason. A "+1" floats off the counter each time it ticks. The villain gets a name tag in its reveal: *the voice that always says "later."*
- **Narration:** every beat has a white line in the bottom bar, e.g. *Every Sunday night, Leo makes the same promise.* / *Four hours of advice. Zero pages read.* / *One missed day became a missed week.* / *180 days. Not one of them felt easy.*
- **Kinetic type:** the truth cards and title pop in word by word (the title letter by letter). Red lines slam in with a shake, and a brush swoosh underlines *stopped asking*.

## v3 revisions: the hook

- **0:00 shock.** An extreme close-up of the slap on the 47th "DAY 1" note. The camera rips back to the whole wall, a giant red **47 RESTARTS** slams in centre screen, then flies into the counter.
- **0:05 the villain talks to you.** It rises out of Leo's shadow and turns to the camera: *"Start Monday."* then *"You know my voice too."* Its first line matches the thumbnail.
- **0:10 curiosity gap.** *"It took him 47 restarts to learn what disciplined people never say out loud."* The truth cards at 1:34 pay it off.
- **Accelerating failures.** After the first two full scenes, the next four play shorter and faster (6 → 5 → 4 → 5 s) and the counter spins.
- **0:50 re-hook.** On the couch, the villain looks at the camera: *"Sound familiar?"*
