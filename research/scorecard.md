# Inspired Daily — 10-point reel scorecard

Each criterion is worth 1 point. A reel ships only at **9.0 or above**.

| # | Criterion | 1 point means |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook at 0s | A specific "that's me" line plus motion in the first second |
| 2 | Relatability | The viewer has lived this exact moment |
| 3 | Payoff density | Every scene lands a turn or transformation |
| 4 | Pacing | Still time ≤ 3.5s and motion ≥ 1.56 (reference bank average) |
| 5 | Visual punch | Fill ≥ 5.6%, a depth stage, big shaded props, sharp text |
| 6 | Text economy | At most 2 lines per scene, about 6 words max, nothing redundant |
| 7 | Arc + climax | Scenes escalate, with a ref-#14 device (counter, typewriter tag, near-miss) and a strong last beat |
| 8 | Shareability | A built-in save, send or tag trigger in both the story and the caption |
| 9 | Rewatch | Humour, detail or a twist that rewards a second watch, and a clean loop |
| 10 | Originality + brand | Not in the ledger, not the reference creator's idea, on-brand look |

**Must-pass gate (before scoring): reference fit.** The theme is discipline, consistency, success or never giving up, told through visual metaphors in a serious tone and building to a climax. Lifestyle tips, comedy and social-anxiety stories fail the gate whatever they score.
