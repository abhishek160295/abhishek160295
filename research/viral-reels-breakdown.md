# Viral Motivation Reels — Breakdown (13 reels)

Source style: "SIMPLY ANIMATED" (watermark visible on every reel, bottom centre).

## Hard numbers
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Length | 30.8s – 38.0s (sweet spot 31–35s) |
| Format | 9:16 vertical, 720x1280, 30fps |
| Hard cuts | 0 in 10 of 13 reels. Scenes change by fade-to-black or object morph |
| Scenes per reel | 4–6 mini-metaphors, ~5–8s each |
| Audio loudness | -5.4 to -6.7 LUFS (extremely loud, clipping past 0 dBFS) |
| Voiceover | None. Music only, zero silence |
| Text per scene | 1–6 words |

## Visual system
- **Background:** pure black. Nothing else.
- **Characters:** white stick figures (circle head, line limbs). No faces, no expressions. Anyone can see themselves in it.
- **Palette:** white/grey only. One accent colour max per scene, used as meaning:
  - Red = problem, failure, past, obsession
  - Green = growth, win
  - Gold/yellow = success, trophy
- **Glow:** soft bloom on key words ("SUCCESS", "WIN") and on the hero object.
- **Ground:** single thin grey line. Sometimes brown soil (plant/tree metaphors) or a dark slope (boulder metaphors).
- **Type:** clean sans (Montserrat/Poppins style), small, centred top. Keywords sometimes in caps.
- **Camera:** static. All motion is in the objects.
- **Level of work:** low-to-mid. 2D vector, simple keyframes, ease-in/out, a few particle bursts (shattering "SUCCESS", glass breaking). Doable in After Effects, Jitter, Rive, Canva, CapCut keyframes or Remotion.

## The formula (every scene follows one of these)
1. **Split comparison** — two rows, same action, different outcome.
   Motivation vs Discipline · Messy action vs Perfection · Consistency vs Intensity · Working hard vs Working smart · Giving up vs Consistency · Fear failure vs Fear judgement · Learning vs Not learning from mistakes · Action vs Words
2. **Time lapse** — Today → In a month → In a year / 1 Week → 9 Months → 1 Year. Small effort becomes big result.
3. **Literal metaphor** — boulder up a hill, ladder, staircase to trophy, tree with hidden roots ("What people see / don't see"), carrot (Confidence) vs leafy top (Ego), seed watered with "Risk" breaking "Comfort Zone".
4. **Text transform** — "I CAN'T DO IT" → hammer smashes the apostrophe → "I CAN DO IT". "Try / Fail / Try again…" stacking → "WIN". "AGAIN AGAIN AGAIN".
5. **Graph** — Days → Months → Decades exponential curve; "A bad day ≠ a bad life" zoom-out stock chart.

## Why it goes viral
- **Instant read, no sound needed.** Message lands in under 2s with sound off.
- **Payoff in every scene.** Each 6s block has a mini twist (the lazy one loses, the consistent one wins). Multiple dopamine hits per reel.
- **Rewatchable.** 4–6 ideas packed into 33s; viewers replay to catch them. Loops cleanly because the ending is just another scene, no outro.
- **Saves + shares.** Content is quotable and "send to a friend" motivational. Saves are the main algorithm fuel here.
- **No face, no voice, no language barrier.** Works globally.
- **Title = a proverb.** "Actions speak louder than words", "Prove them wrong". Familiar, searchable, comment-bait.

## Sound
- Trending high-energy track (phonk / epic cinematic / gym edit style), added as platform audio.
- Clear **beat switch near the midpoint** (spectrogram shows energy jump around ~50–60%). Scene changes and payoffs are timed to beats.
- Mastered very loud. No SFX layer needed, though hammer hits and shatters land on kicks.

## Production template (to copy the format, not the content)
1. Hook (0–1s): scene already moving, label text visible on frame 1. No intro, no logo.
2. Scenes 2–5: 5–7s each, fade to black between.
3. Strongest metaphor last.
4. Caption: the proverb + 3–5 hashtags (#motivation #discipline #mindset #success #selfimprovement).
5. Post daily, same time. Keep the look identical so the feed reads as one brand.
