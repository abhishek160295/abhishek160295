## #14 — "Make it happen" (added 24 Oct)
Same creator (SIMPLY ANIMATED). It's the best-performing file on our metrics: motion 2.42 (highest in the bank) and zero still time.

It has 4 scenes of about 8s each, instead of 5–6 short ones:
1. **Multitasking vs focus.** One figure digs many shallow holes, the other digs one deep hole that strikes glowing gold.
2. **Your words vs your actions.** On a hanging scale, a pile of small "words" blocks is outweighed by one gold "actions" block.
3. **Motivated vs disciplined.** Two gym rows run with DAY 001 → 365 counters and a flipping calendar. The motivated one stalls, the disciplined one keeps lifting.
4. **The power of consistency.** A crowd pushes 3D boulders uphill and drops out one by one with typed labels: TOO HARD, NO TIME, NOT WORTH IT, THEY LAUGHED. One reaches the top as the sun reveals "THIS CLOSE".

What it adds to our picture of the style:
- **Longer scenes that build.** Each scene is a small story with escalation, not a single beat.
- **Depth.** Mid-grey gradient terrain, 3D-shaded boulders and soft vignettes instead of pure flat black.
- **Typewriter labels.** Mono text inside small boxes ("TOO HARD") stuck onto characters.
- **Counters as tension.** DAY 001 → 365 and a flipping calendar to show time passing.
- **A near-miss climax.** "THIS CLOSE" turns the quitters into the lesson. It's emotional and gets screenshotted.
- **3 of 4 scenes are side-by-side comparisons**, the same pattern as the rest of the bank.
