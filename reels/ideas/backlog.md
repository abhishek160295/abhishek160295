# Idea backlog

Each line is topic, then the visual metaphor. Remove an idea once it's produced. Keep adding new ones.

## Split comparison
- Talker vs Doer: speech bubbles pile up vs bricks stack into a wall
- Consumer vs Creator: one figure absorbs falling squares and stays the same; the other emits light and grows
- Busy vs Productive: one runs on a treadmill and goes nowhere; the other walks 3 steps and reaches the flag
- Waiting vs Starting: waits for a green light that never comes vs walks while it's red and finds a path
- Perfect vs Done: sanding one block forever vs shipping 5 rough blocks that form a bridge
- Reacting vs Responding: a ball bouncing chaotically vs a ball absorbing the hit and rolling forward
- Excuses vs Results: a figure builds a wall of excuse blocks between themselves and the flag
- Scroll vs Build: a thumb scrolls endless lines vs the same lines stack into a tower
- Rest vs Quit: battery recharges and continues vs battery unplugs and dims forever
- Amateur vs Pro: practices until they get it right vs until they can't get it wrong (one hit vs 10 in a row)
- Effort vs Leverage: pushing a boulder vs placing a lever and tipping it with one finger
- Comparison vs Focus: one figure keeps looking at the other lane and trips; the other looks ahead

## Percentage ladder
- 99% vs 1% habits: three archers, only the one who keeps re-drawing hits
- Who finishes a book: 100 dots start, chapter markers, 10% reach the last page
- New Year's resolutions: dots fade month by month from Jan to Dec

## Curve or math
- Plateau of latent potential: expectation line vs reality curve that's below for a while, then crosses above
- Iceberg of success: the tip says "success", below: failures, discipline, sacrifice, habits
- Pareto: 20% of the bars glow and fill 80% of the meter
- Time vs money: one line goes down while the other goes up

## Transformation
- Square to circle: friction slowly rounds a square (each repetition sands an edge) until it rolls
- Seed: watering every day, nothing above ground for 6 days, roots growing below, sprout on day 7
- Battery of discipline: each "no" to a distraction adds a bar
- Dark room: each habit lights one bulb until the room is bright

## Metaphor line
- "Pressure makes diamonds": a carbon dot compressed until it becomes a glowing diamond
- "Rivers cut rock with persistence, not power": water lines carving a canyon over time
- "You are the average of the 5 people you spend time with": the centre dot's colour blends with its neighbours
- "Comfort is a beautiful place, but nothing grows there": a flower in a sealed glass dome stays a bud
