# The Story Behind the Reels

Each reel is a parable told in about 12 seconds. A truth you already know but don't act on gets turned into something physical that you can watch happen. The point is never to teach you something new. The point is to make you see it.

## The Core Engine

> **Make an invisible truth visible, then let time play out the consequence.**

Habits, attention, energy, discipline and fear have no shape. These reels give each one a body, an object and a physical law, then speed up time so you see the result in seconds instead of years.

| Invisible truth | Made visible as |
|---|---|
| Energy around people | A battery that drains or charges |
| Attention | Gravity that pulls a galaxy together |
| Your past | A chain dragging you away from success |
| Setbacks | An arrow drawn backward before it flies |
| Effort that hasn't paid off yet | 99 hits on a rock that seems unchanged |
| Perfectionism | Polishing one brick forever |
| What you consume | What enters the head: a book's glow or a phone's drain |
| Time left | Dots on a calendar disappearing |
| The system | A pipe carrying profit up and salary down |
| Comfort | The red inner circle you never leave |

## The 6-Beat Story

Every reel follows this arc, even the wordless ones:

1. **Mirror:** Two figures, the same body and the same starting point. One of them is you. Nobody is born ahead.
2. **The choice:** A single small difference. Jump or think. Swing or stop. Lay a brick or polish one. Wake up or snooze.
3. **Compression:** Time speeds up. Small acts pile up: bricks, hits, minutes, days.
4. **The gap:** The difference becomes huge and impossible to ignore. Wall versus one brick. Galaxy versus empty grid. Gold versus drips.
5. **The reversal:** What you expected flips. Motivation sprints ahead, then Discipline passes it. The quitter was one hit away. The crowd that mocked now cheers. The overthinker never jumps.
6. **The verdict:** A command of 1 to 6 words. *Start. Don't. Keep swinging. Don't follow blindly.*

## Why It Hits

- **Recognition before judgment.** The viewer sees themselves in the losing figure first. It stings, but softly, because it's a faceless stick figure and not a person lecturing them.
- **No villain.** The loser isn't bad, just stuck. Being stuck feels fixable, and that makes the reel hopeful instead of preachy.
- **One small fix.** The winner never does anything heroic. They do something tiny, repeatedly. It feels doable today.
- **Proof, not opinion.** You watch the wall go up, so you can't argue with it. The visual is the argument.
- **Faceless and universal.** No race, age, language or brand. Anyone, anywhere, can project themselves onto the figure.
- **Silence carries it.** It works with the sound off. Music only sets the emotion: slowed, cinematic, introspective.
- **Rewatch loop.** It's short and dense, and it ends where it began, so people watch it twice and share it with a friend: "this is you."

## The Five Recurring Themes

1. **Action over thought:** Overthinker vs Doer, Perfectionist vs Action Taker, Think → Write → Prioritize → Start
2. **Consistency over intensity:** Motivation vs Discipline, 99 vs 100 hits, 5 minutes a day
3. **Inputs shape outputs:** what you consume, who drains you, where your attention goes
4. **Self over crowd:** blindfolded crowd off a cliff, the mocking crowd that later cheers, 93% / 6% / 1%
5. **Time is running out:** 91 days left, 13 weekends, "Most people will wait for January"

## The Blueprint for a New Story

```
TRUTH:     [habit or mindset everyone knows but ignores]
OBJECT:    [physical thing that makes it visible]
A vs B:    [same figure, one tiny different choice]
TIME:      [what accumulates: bricks, hits, days, light]
REVERSAL:  [the expected winner loses, or the loser was 1 step away]
VERDICT:   [1–6 word command]
```

Example: TRUTH = comparison steals joy. OBJECT = each figure carries a lamp. A watches others' lamps while their own dims; B tends their own lamp and it grows into a sun. REVERSAL: the brightest lamp A envied was only a reflection of B's. VERDICT: *Tend your own light.*
