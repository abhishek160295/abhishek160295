# Minimal Motivation Reels — Style Playbook

Distilled from 20 reference reels (neuronvisuals, mindset.therapy, adoslabs, thequietfixx, soulmatevisuals). This is the blueprint for producing reels in the same genre.

## 1. The Formula

One idea. One metaphor. One contrast. 5–17 seconds. Loop-ready.

| Beat | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–1s | Label or question on screen instantly. No intro, no logo. |
| Setup | 1–4s | Two figures start identical. |
| Divergence | 4–10s | One grows, one stalls. Visual cost of the wrong choice. |
| Punchline | 10–14s | 2–6 word verdict text. |
| End card | last 1–2s | Scene fades, centered IG logo + handle. |

## 2. Recurring Structures

1. **Split-screen A vs B** — Overthinker/Doer, Perfectionist/Action Taker, Quits at 99/Hits 100, Motivated/Unmotivated, 5 Minutes/No Time, Employee/Management.
2. **Percentage tiers** — 93% / 6% / 1% of people, each row a progressively better outcome.
3. **Single metaphor build** — attention = gravity forming a galaxy; life = arrow pulled back; social battery draining.
4. **Data countdown** — dot-matrix calendar: "How much of 2026 is left?" → 91 days → 13 weeks → 13 weekends → "Most people will wait for January. Don't. Start."
5. **Step sequence** — Think → Write → Prioritize → Start, one word per scene.
6. **Crowd vs individual** — blindfolded crowd walks off a cliff; one stops. "Don't follow blindly."
7. **Concentric zones** — Comfort → Fear → Learning → Growth → Success.

## 3. Proven Concepts Bank

- Overthinker vs Doer (diving board)
- Perfectionist polishes one brick; Action Taker builds a wall
- Hits the rock 99 times and quits vs hits 100: "You never know which hit breaks it. Keep swinging."
- Motivation sprints and fades; Discipline jogs and finishes
- Discipline is doing it unmotivated
- Your past chained to You, dragging away from Success
- Watch what you consume (books glow, phone drains)
- Profit pipe: salary drips to employee, gold pours to management
- Your social battery is limited. Be mindful of who drains it and who charges it.
- Motivation / Discipline / Obsession (pull-up bars) → dictionary card "obsessed"
- 5 minutes push-ups / eggs / shower vs snooze / donut / no shower
- Work → Learn → Build → Grow → Success staircase; the crowd that mocked now cheers
- Your attention has gravity

## 4. Visual System

**Palette**
- Base: pure black `#000` to charcoal `#1A1A1A`, subtle radial vignette
- Figures: white `#F5F5F5` stick figures with soft glow
- Single accent per reel: red `#E5322D` (danger, loss), gold `#F2C200` (success, discipline), cyan-blue `#3FA9F5` (calm, focus), neon green `#2BFF88` (win), violet `#A86BFF` (progress)

**Figures**
- Round-head stick figures, thick limbs, no face (or minimal eyes)
- Floor reflection on glossy black ground
- 3D-lit, slightly extruded, soft rim light

**Typography**
- Labels: small uppercase pixel/monospace, wide tracking (OVERTHINKER, QUITS AT 99)
- Headlines: bold condensed sans (5 MINUTES., START 2027)
- Captions: clean sans, lowercase, center bottom, word-by-word reveal
- Never more than 8 words on screen at once

**Composition**
- 9:16, 1080×1920
- Top 25%: label text. Middle 50%: action. Bottom: caption + reflection.
- Handle watermark top-right, small, low opacity
- Center-weighted, generous negative space

## 5. Motion & Sound

- Ease-in-out, no hard cuts inside a scene
- Glow pulses on key moments (target hit, battery recharge)
- Counters tick up/down (2 → 59 → 99 → 100)
- Particles and light trails for "growth" moments
- Audio: slowed + reverb instrumental, phonk, or cinematic ambient (Hans Zimmer style). Song credit under handle.
- One sound accent at the punchline (impact, chime, crack)

## 6. Copy Rules

- Labels are nouns: DOER, PERFECTIONIST, EMPLOYEE
- Punchlines are imperative or absolute: "Keep swinging." "Don't." "Start."
- Use real numbers: 91 days, 13 weekends, 1%, 99 hits
- No hashtags, no emojis on screen
- Caption post text: 1 line hook + 1 line question to drive comments

## 7. Production Stack

| Need | Tool |
|---|---|
| 3D stick figures & scenes | Blender (free), Cinema 4D |
| 2D motion + text | After Effects, CapCut, Jitter |
| Code-driven (dot grids, counters) | Remotion, HTML/Canvas |
| Glow & neon | AE Deep Glow, CapCut glow effect |
| Music | IG/TikTok trending library, slowed instrumentals |

## 8. Pre-Publish Checklist

- [ ] Hook readable in first frame
- [ ] One idea, one accent color
- [ ] Under 17 seconds
- [ ] Contrast obvious with sound off
- [ ] Punchline ≤ 6 words
- [ ] End card with handle
- [ ] Loops cleanly back to frame 1
