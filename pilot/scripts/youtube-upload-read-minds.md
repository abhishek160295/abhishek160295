## Pinned comment

The real science in this one:

- Scientists at the University of Texas built an AI that turns brain scans into sentences (Tang et al., Nature Neuroscience, 2023). It needed about 16 hours of scans per person, and a decoder trained on one brain didn't work on anyone else.
- In the same study, people could block it just by thinking about something else on purpose, like counting or naming animals. Counting by sevens works.
- Your inner voice isn't on all the time. When researchers sampled random moments of people's thoughts, actual words showed up only about a quarter of the time (Hurlburt's experience sampling studies).
- Some people have little or no inner voice at all. It was named anendophasia in 2024 (Nedergaard & Lupyan).
- The cocktail party effect, picking one voice out of a noisy room, was described by Colin Cherry in 1953.
- Every time you recall a memory, your brain rebuilds it, and it can change a little each time.
- Dogs can tell happy human faces from angry ones, follow where we point and look, and their brains react to emotion in our voices.
