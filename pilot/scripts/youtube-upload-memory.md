## Pinned comment

The real science behind this one:

- The forgetting curve: Hermann Ebbinghaus, 1885. Without review, most new material is gone within a day.
- Solomon Shereshevsky, studied for about 30 years by Alexander Luria (The Mind of a Mnemonist). He recalled lists 15+ years later and struggled with faces and abstract meaning.
- Hyperthymesia (HSAM): first described in 2006 (Parker, Cahill, McGaugh). Jill Price called her memory "nonstop, uncontrollable, and totally exhausting". Only a few dozen confirmed cases.
- Forgetting helps you think: Richards & Frankland, Neuron, 2017.
- Brain storage of ~2.5 petabytes is an estimate (Paul Reber), not a measurement.
