## Pinned comment

The maths, for anyone who wants to check us:

- Max is 70 kg. Shoe grip on a road is about 0.8 of body weight, so the most his feet can push forward is about 550 N.
- Air drag = ½ × air density × drag area × speed². With a drag area of 0.4 m², drag hits 550 N at about 172 km/h. That's his real top speed on flat ground.
- Power to push air goes up with speed cubed. 50x the speed = 125,000x the power. At 1,250 km/h that's about 10 MW.
- Muscles are roughly 25% efficient. At top speed he burns ~120 kW, about 600 kcal per km.
- About 75% of that becomes heat, ~90 kW. Body heat capacity is ~245 kJ per °C, so he warms ~0.36 °C per second. Sweating clears ~1 kW at best.
- Stopping from 172 km/h with grip plus air drag: ~100 m. Turning circle: ~580 m.
- High-five: ½ × 0.5 kg × (250 m/s)² ≈ 16 kJ. A .50 cal round is about 18 kJ.
- Jump: takeoff 50x normal (135 m/s), with drag, tops out near 290 m. The Eiffel Tower's top deck is 276 m.
- The sprint study: Weyand et al., Journal of Applied Physiology, 2010 (Southern Methodist University).
