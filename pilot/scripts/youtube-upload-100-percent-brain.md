## Pinned comment

The real science in this one:

- Your brain has roughly 86 billion neurons (Azevedo et al., 2009).
- It's about 2% of your body weight but uses about 20% of your energy at rest, roughly 20 watts.
- Your eyes send the brain millions of bits of information every second. Estimates of how much you consciously notice are far smaller. Exact numbers vary by study.
- Every eye has a blind spot where the optic nerve leaves it. Your brain fills in the gap, so you never notice it.
- Kim Peek could read two pages at once, one with each eye, and remembered thousands of books.
- Seeing faces in clouds, toast and plug sockets is called pareidolia. Everyone does it.
- The "we only use 10% of our brain" idea is a myth. Nobody is sure where it started. Brain scans show you use pretty much all of it over a day, just not all at once.
- A seizure happens when large groups of neurons fire together abnormally. Around 50 million people worldwide live with epilepsy (WHO), and treatment helps most of them.
