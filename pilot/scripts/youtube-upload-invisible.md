## Pinned comment

The real science behind this one:

- To be invisible, light has to pass through you without being absorbed or bent. Your retina only works by absorbing light, so a truly invisible eye can't see. H.G. Wells's The Invisible Man (1897) skips this.
- That's why every see-through animal (glass frogs, glass squid, eel larvae) still has visible eyes.
- Glass and water are see-through but visible because they bend light. To vanish, your body would have to bend light exactly like air.
- Undigested food would show inside an invisible body. Wells used this too.
- Every warm body gives off infrared, so thermal cameras and pit vipers would still see you.
- Some blind people navigate by echolocation, clicking and listening to echoes. Some ride bikes this way.
- About 1.19 million people die on the world's roads every year (WHO, 2023).
