FORMULAS

A calculator that never gets tired.

How much money did you make from one sale?

=C2*D212 cups × ₹20 = ₹240

Sell 20 cups instead of 12? Change C2 and the answer updates by itself.

Every formula starts with =. That's how Excel knows it's maths.

SUM · AVERAGE

Counting your piggy bank.

Total money this week:

=SUM(E2:E8)Adds every box from E2 down to E8

A normal day looks like:

=AVERAGE(E2:E8)Total ÷ number of days

EXPLAIN LIKE I'M 506/11
IF

Excel can make decisions.

"If I sold more than 10 cups, it was a great day. Otherwise, slow day."

=IF(C2>10,"Great","Slow")Question → answer if yes → answer if no

Companies use this exact logic to flag late orders, low stock and VIP customers.

EXPLAIN LIKE I'M 507/11
XLOOKUP

Asking mom for the price list.

Prices are stuck on the fridge. You don't remember them. You just ask.

=XLOOKUP(B2,Menu!A:A,Menu!B:B)"Find Lemon on the menu. Bring me its price."

This one formula replaces hours of copy-paste. Interviewers always ask about it.
